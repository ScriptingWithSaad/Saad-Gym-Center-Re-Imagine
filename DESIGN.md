# Design direction

A contemporary training club: confident athletic typography, real training photography and clear choices. Keep the eight existing programs and three existing monthly plans. Remove mismatched trainer identities and placeholder contact information; enquiries go to the owner's supplied profiles.

- Palette: club navy #132A3B; cobalt #2459DF; white #FFFFFF; cool canvas #F3F6F8; mist #E7EFF5; slate #566674.
- Type: Barlow Condensed 700/800 for strong, compact display headlines; Manrope for navigation and readable body copy. Self-host the fonts.
- Layout: a spacious white navigation above a navy split hero; four photographic program previews with filters; an asymmetrical coaching feature; a three-plan comparison; quiet FAQ rows; a navy invitation and complete footer.
- Alignment: content and headings left aligned, pricing benefits easy to compare; mobile retains the same reading order.
- Distinctive element: the oversized three-line hero beside a full-height battle-rope training photograph. Keep the remaining components restrained.

Review: avoid the generic black-and-neon bodybuilding theme, invented statistics, repetitive card sections and decorative animation. The blue/navy club identity and photography do the visual work. Pricing cards and class previews repeat because they are directly comparable content. All controls work, motion is optional, and contact actions never claim an enquiry has been sent automatically.
