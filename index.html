Before your first session

GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Still deciding? Let’s talk.

Is this a good place to start as a beginner?+

Absolutely. You don’t need to arrive with an established routine. Tell us where you’re starting, and ask about an initial fitness assessment and suitable classes before your first visit.

Which membership should I choose?+

Basic covers independent gym training. Premium adds group classes and a personal training session. Elite includes unlimited classes and four personal training sessions per month. The team can help you compare what fits your routine.

Can I get help with my training?+

Yes. Personal coaching is included in the Premium and Elite plans. Ask the team about available coaches, session times and the support you’re looking for.

How do I join or arrange a visit?+

Choose a membership or use “Talk to us” below. You can prepare an enquiry and contact us on Instagram. We’ll confirm the details and availability before you make any payment or booking.
